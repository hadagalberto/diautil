# diautil — SPEC

**diautil**: Brazilian holidays and business-day calendar for TypeScript/JavaScript — national, state and capital-city holidays,
banking calendar (FEBRABAN rules), business-day arithmetic (D+N, prazos, settlement dates), with sources for every rule. Plus a CLI.
Open source, MIT, "Hadagalberto Junior". NOT a toy: every holiday rule cites its legal source (law/decree number) in code comments and docs;
no stubs, no TODO, no placeholder/skipped tests.

## Stack
TypeScript 5 strict, zero runtime deps, ESM+CJS (tsup), works in browsers/Deno/Bun/Node ≥ 20, tree-shakeable data per state. Vitest (coverage ≥ 95% lines),
fast-check property tests, ESLint strict + Prettier, GitHub Actions CI (node 20/22/24). README.md + README.pt-BR.md, CHANGELOG, CONTRIBUTING, LICENSE. Do not publish.

## Date model
- Use plain calendar dates, never `Date` timezone math internally: `type PlainDate = { year, month, day }` + helpers; accept `Date` (interpreted in America/Sao_Paulo by default, configurable)
  and ISO strings `YYYY-MM-DD` at the API boundary. Proleptic Gregorian, valid years 1900–2199. Day-of-week computed arithmetically.

## Holidays
- **Easter**: Anonymous Gregorian algorithm (Meeus/Jones/Butcher). Tests against a table of known Easter dates (e.g. 2024-03-31, 2025-04-20, 2026-04-05, 2027-03-28, 2030-04-21, 2038-04-25, 1961-04-02, 2100-03-28).
  Derived movable dates: Carnaval Monday/Tuesday (Easter −48/−47), Ash Wednesday (−46, optional "until 14h"), Good Friday (−2), Corpus Christi (+60).
- **National holidays** with year-validity rules (Lei 662/1949, Lei 6.802/1980 (Aparecida), Lei 10.607/2002, **Lei 14.759/2023 — Dia Nacional de Zumbi e da Consciência Negra, 20/11, national holiday from 2024**),
  each rule with `since`/`until` years. Distinguish `type: 'national' | 'state' | 'municipal' | 'optional'` (ponto facultativo: Carnaval, Ash Wednesday morning, Corpus Christi for federal public service).
- **State holidays** for all 27 UFs (e.g. SP 09/07 Revolução Constitucionalista; RJ 23/04 São Jorge; etc.), each with its state law citation. Where you cannot confirm a state holiday and
  its law from an official source (state legislature/government site), do not include it — list it in `docs/unverified.md` instead. Fetching official sources is allowed.
- **Capital cities** municipal holidays for the 27 capitals (e.g. São Paulo 25/01 aniversário), same verification rule.
- Moving-holiday rules expressed declaratively (fixed date, Easter offset, nth weekday of month, "moved to Monday" rules when a law says so).
- API: `holidays(year, { uf?, city?, include?: types[] })`, `isHoliday(date, opts)`, `holidayName(date, opts)` (pt-BR and en names), `nextHoliday(date, opts)`.

## Business days
- `isBusinessDay(date, opts)`, `addBusinessDays(date, n, opts)` (n may be negative; n=0 rolls forward/backward per `roll` option),
  `businessDaysBetween(a, b, { inclusive })`, `nextBusinessDay`, `previousBusinessDay`, `lastBusinessDayOfMonth`, `nthBusinessDayOfMonth` (e.g. 5º dia útil — salário, CLT art. 459).
- Weekends configurable (default Sat/Sun). Custom calendars: `createCalendar({ uf, city, extraHolidays, removeHolidays })`.
- **Banking calendar** (`calendar: 'bancario'`): national holidays + Carnaval Mon/Tue as non-business days for interbank settlement, per FEBRABAN/BCB practice; document the source.
  Settlement helpers: `boletoDueDateAdjust` (vencimento em dia não útil → próximo dia útil, CMN Resolução/FEBRABAN rule cited), D+1/D+2 settlement.
- Performance: precomputed per-year caches; `businessDaysBetween` across 100 years < 5 ms.

## CLI `diautil`
`diautil feriados 2026 --uf SP --cidade "São Paulo" [--json]`, `diautil util 2026-11-20 --uf RJ`, `diautil soma 2026-11-19 10 --calendario bancario`,
`diautil entre 2026-01-01 2026-12-31 --uf MG`, `diautil quinto-dia-util 2026-12`. pt-BR output, `--json` machine output.

## Tests

- Specific regression tests: 20/11/2023 NOT national; 20/11/2024 national; Carnaval 2026 = 16–17/02; Corpus Christi 2026 = 04/06; Good Friday 2026 = 03/04.
- Property tests: addBusinessDays(addBusinessDays(d, n), -n) round-trips on business days; businessDaysBetween consistent with addBusinessDays.
